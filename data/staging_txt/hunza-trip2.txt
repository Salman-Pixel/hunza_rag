 10 DAYS TOUR TO 
HUNZA & NALTAR VALLEY

Eventica Travels® presents a chance to witness two of the most beautiful valleys in our 10 days 
tour this summer with your families and friends.

has three famous lakes. 

Also, Hunza Valley is the most famous tourist spot in Pakistan with its famous hospitality and 
huge Karakoram Range Mountains and many others are added to the trip to make it worthwhile 
for the participants.

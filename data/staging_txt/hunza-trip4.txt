Day 1 Arrival Islamabad 

• Arrive at Islamabad International Airport. 
• Meet our representative and transfer to hotel. 
• Check in and overnight stay in Islamabad.

• Breakfast at 07:00am. 
• At 08:30am, visit Golra Railway Station and Railway Museum. 
• Depart to Besham. 
• Arrive at Besham, check in and overnight stay. 

Day 3 Besham – Gilgit (Breakfast/Dinner)

• 07:30am, depart to Gilgit.

• Continue travelling to Gilgit and overnight stay at Gilgit.

• 08:30am check out from hotel and proceed to Hunza. 
• Short stop at Rakaposhi View Point. 
• Arrive at Hunza. 
• 12:30 lunch time and prayer break. 
• 3:30pm go to Eagles Nest View Point to enjoy sunset. 
• Return to hotel. 
• 08:00pm dinner and overnight stay.
